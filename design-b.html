FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need a permit?

Usually, and California has made ADU permits much easier. Rules vary by city and county. We'll tell you what your building department typically needs.

What foundation?

A level slab, concrete piers or a compacted gravel pad sized to the expanded footprint. We advise per site.

Utilities?

Pre-wired and pre-plumbed. Your electrician connects 240 V; your plumber connects water and sewer or septic. Off-grid with solar and a tank works too.

Is delivery included?

Yes. Unit, delivery and setup are quoted together for your zip code.
