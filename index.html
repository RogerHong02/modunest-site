FAQ

Questions people ask before they order

More answers on the FAQ page, or just call us.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on your city and county and on how you'll use the unit. Many California customers use these homes as ADUs, which most jurisdictions now allow on residential lots. We'll help you understand what your local building department requires before you order.

What kind of foundation do I need?

A level, compacted surface: a concrete slab, concrete piers or a gravel pad. We'll advise on the best option for your site when we quote.

How do water and power connect?

Each home is pre-wired and pre-plumbed. Your electrician and plumber connect it to your existing 240 V service and water and sewer lines, or you can go off-grid with solar and a tank.

Really two hours?

Yes, for the setup itself. Once the truck arrives, our crew places the unit, expands both wings and locks it in place in about two hours. Utility hookups are done separately by your local trades.

Is delivery included in the price?

We quote delivery and installation together by zip code, so the number you see is the number you pay. No surprises on delivery day.

Can I buy without installation?

In California we deliver and set up every unit. For other states, ask us about freight-only options.
