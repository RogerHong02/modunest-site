Delivery & installation

One truck, one crew, one price

We don't drop a container in your driveway and leave. Delivery and setup are part of every California order.

Delivery

Flatbed transport from our Southern California yard to your address. Quoted by zip code, included in your price.

Setup

Our crew offloads, positions, expands and locks the unit, then checks every door and window. About two hours.

Furniture (optional)

Beds, dining set, sofa and coffee table matched to your model, delivered and placed with the home.

Site requirements

What your site needs

A level pad – concrete slab, concrete piers or compacted gravel, sized to the expanded footprint of your model
Truck access – a path about 12 ft wide with 14 ft of overhead clearance for the flatbed
Room to expand – the wings open to over 20 ft wide, so keep that width clear of fences and trees
Utilities nearby – water, sewer or septic, and a 240 V electrical feed; or plan for solar and a tank

Not sure? Send photos of the spot and the route in. We'll tell you if it works before you pay anything.

Hookups

Connecting your home

Every unit is pre-wired and pre-plumbed at the factory. The bathroom, kitchen sink, lights and outlets are already in. Your licensed electrician and plumber make the final connections to your property, the same way they would for a manufactured home.

Off-grid lot? Customers pair the 12 kW design load with a solar array and battery, and use a water tank with a septic or composting system.

We include a hookup guide with every delivery so your trades know exactly where the connection points are.
