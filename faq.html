FAQ

Questions and straight answers

Can't find yours? Call (951) 578-7706 or send us a note.

The home

What is an expandable container home?

A steel-framed home built in a factory to the width of a shipping container, with two side "wings" that fold in for transport and fold out on site. Folded it is 7 ft 3 in wide; expanded it is over 20 ft wide. Kitchen, bathroom, flooring, lighting, windows and doors are installed before it ships.

How is it insulated?

Walls and roof are insulated sandwich panels on a galvanized steel frame, with double-glazed aluminum windows. It's a real four-season building, not a shed.

Can I change the layout?

The layouts shown are our standard configurations. Ask us about your needs when you request a quote and we'll tell you what's possible for the next shipment.

Does it come furnished?

Furniture is an optional package. The photos on this site show units with the package installed.

Are the materials up to US standards?

Yes. Our units are built with materials selected to meet US standards. Ask us for details relevant to your project and jurisdiction.

Site and permits

Do I need a permit?

Usually yes if you'll live in it or rent it out, and the rules differ by city and county. California has made ADUs much easier to permit in recent years. We'll point you to what your local building department needs and share what other customers have done.

What foundation is required?

A level, compacted surface: a concrete slab, concrete piers or a gravel pad sized to the expanded footprint. We'll advise for your site and soil.

How do utilities connect?

Each home is pre-wired and pre-plumbed. Your electrician connects a 240 V feed; your plumber connects water and sewer or septic. Off-grid setups with solar and a tank are common on rural lots.

Delivery and setup

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in California, with delivery and installation included. Chicago is coming soon. For other states, ask about freight-only delivery.

Is it really set up in two hours?

The on-site setup, yes: offload, position, expand both wings, lock the panels, check doors and windows. Utility hookups by your trades are separate.

What access does the truck need?

A path about 12 ft wide and 14 ft high for a flatbed, and space next to the pad to offload. Send us photos and we'll confirm before you order.

How long until delivery?

Units are sold from each California shipment in order of reservation. Ask us for the current delivery window when you request a quote.

Pricing and ordering

How much does it cost?

We quote each model with delivery and installation for your zip code, so you get one complete number. Request a quote and we'll reply within one business day.

How do I reserve one?

Request a quote, confirm your model and site, and place a deposit. Your unit is assigned from the next shipment in the order reservations come in.

Do you offer financing?

Ask us. We can point you to financing options used by other customers for ADUs and modular homes.

Who are you?

ModuNest Technologies LLC is a US company with operations in Southern California. We import, deliver and install expandable homes built by our manufacturing partner, which has produced modular buildings since 2001.
