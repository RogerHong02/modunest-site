About

We sell the whole experience, not a box

Most container homes online are sold by factories overseas: you get a crate at the port and figure out the rest. We started ModuNest to do the opposite.

What we do

Delivered, set up, ready to live in

ModuNest Technologies LLC is a US company with operations in Southern California. We keep units in stock locally, deliver them to your property on our own schedule, and set them up with our own crew. If you want it furnished, we do that too.

The homes are built by our manufacturing partner, which has produced modular buildings since 2001. We chose the expandable design because it solves the two things that make ADUs painful: time and coordination. One truck, one crew, about two hours.

We're starting in California, where the need for extra housing is obvious and the ADU rules have caught up. Chicago is next.
