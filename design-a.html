Good to know

Before you order

Do I need a permit for a backyard unit?

Usually yes, and California has made ADU permits much easier in recent years. Rules differ by city and county. We'll tell you what your building department typically needs and share how other customers handled it.

What does my yard need?

A level pad (slab, piers or compacted gravel), room for the wings to open to about 20 ft wide, and a path about 12 ft wide for the flatbed. Send photos and we'll confirm before you pay anything.

Is delivery really included?

Yes. We quote the unit, delivery and setup together for your zip code. One number.

How fast is "two hours"?

That's the on-site setup: offload, open both wings, lock, check doors and windows. Your electrician and plumber connect utilities separately.
