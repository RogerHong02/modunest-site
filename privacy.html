Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the information you enter: name, phone number, email address, zip code and the details of your project. We also receive standard web server data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use it

To reply to your request, prepare a price, plan delivery and follow up on your project. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to marketing lists without asking.

Advertising and analytics

This site may use Google Ads and Google Analytics, which set cookies to measure visits and conversions. You can control cookies in your browser settings and opt out of Google's ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Sharing

We share your information only with service providers who help us run this site and deliver your order, such as our form service, transport and installation crews, and only as needed for that purpose.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@modunest.example. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA; contact us to exercise them.
