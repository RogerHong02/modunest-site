How it works

From first call to first night in

No construction site, no trades to schedule. Here's exactly what happens.

1

Get a delivered price

Send us your zip code, the model you're considering and how you'll use it. We reply with a price that includes the unit, delivery and setup. If you're unsure about size, we'll recommend one.

2

Check permits and prepare the site

We'll tell you what your city or county typically requires and how other customers have handled it. On site you need a level pad (slab, piers or compacted gravel) and access for a flatbed truck. We confirm access with photos or a quick call.

3

Reserve your unit

Units are sold from each California shipment in order of reservation. A deposit holds yours. We confirm the delivery window with you as the shipment arrives.

4

Delivery day: about two hours

The unit arrives folded, 7 ft wide, on a flatbed. Our crew offloads it onto your pad, expands both wings, locks the panels, checks doors and windows, and cleans up. Most setups take about two hours.

5

Hook up and move in

Your electrician connects the 240 V feed and your plumber connects water and sewer (or a tank and septic). Furniture, if ordered, is delivered with the unit. Then it's yours.

Delivery day

What we need from you on the day

Someone 18 or older on site to sign off
Clear access for a flatbed: about 12 ft wide, no low branches or wires under 14 ft
Pad finished and level before delivery
Pets and vehicles out of the way

Tight lots and hillside sites are common in California. Send us photos and we'll plan the placement with you.
